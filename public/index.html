Wizard Arena

Build your wizard from any spells: one per slot. Four wizards, first to 10 kills.

Loading wizard…

WASD move   Mouse look   LMB RMB R E F Q your six spells   Space jump   C / Ctrl crouch   Shift sprint
R your shield: hold a Ward (blocks spells from the front, drains stamina), or tap the one you picked (an ice wall, a wind veil, a firewall...)
F Telekinesis: aim at an object to lift it, F again to hurl it. It shields you while held. Q your ultimate, once charged
Don't hit the goats. An angry goat charges and kills in one hit, unless your ward (R) faces it
LMB fires a magazine, then reloads (T to reload early). The last shot stuns and breaks a wind-up
G set it down   Tab scoreboard   M mute   Esc menu (and your loadout)
