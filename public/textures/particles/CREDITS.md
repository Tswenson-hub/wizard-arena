# Particle credits

All textures in this folder are from **Kenney's Particle Pack** (www.kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack), licence **CC0 1.0**
(public domain; credit given anyway). Provided by Ty as `kenney_particle-pack.zip`.

Taken from the pack's `PNG (Transparent)` set, 33 of its 80 sprites: downscaled with Lanczos to 256 px (the soft, large ones:
`smoke_01`, `smoke_07`, `smoke_08`, `smoke_10`, `magic_01`, `magic_02`, `scorch_01`, `twirl_01`) or 128 px (the rest), and
saved as grey + alpha PNGs. `magic_01` and `magic_02` have their strokes thickened (a 9 px dilation at 512 px before the
downscale), so the rune circles survive mipmapping on the ground.
